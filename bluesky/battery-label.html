BlueSky battery label

Adhesive label for unbranded batteries, 100 × 72 mm. Group size and CCA are the two things a customer looks for, so they carry the layout; everything else supports them. Edit the CONFIG block at the top of this file to generate the label for a different battery.

1 · Actual size

Check the scale before sending to print. The label is drawn in millimetres, so a printout at 100% (no “fit to page”) measures exactly 100 × 72 mm. Use the ruler above to confirm — if the 100 mm mark lands anywhere else, the print scaling is off, not the file.

2 · Applied to the battery

+ −

Case drawn at group-35 proportions — 230 mm wide, 225 mm tall including the terminal deck. Terminal positions here are illustrative; which side the positive post sits on varies by group, so do not use this as a polarity reference.

Centred on the front face and sitting slightly high, which leaves clear case below for a hold-down bracket and keeps the label off the moulded lip under the deck. Apply it to a dry, degreased face and roll it down from the centre outwards — a corner that never bonded properly is how these start peeling in a Calgary winter.

3 · Print specification

Material White polymeric vinyl, acid resistant, permanent adhesive.
Ask for a battery-grade or chemical-resistant face stock. Standard paper stock lifts within weeks under the hood.
Lamination Matte anti-UV overlaminate. Matte, not gloss — an engine bay is read at an angle under a droplight, and gloss throws the reflection straight back at whoever is looking.
Trim size 100 × 72 mm, corners rounded 4 mm radius.
Bleed & safety Add 3 mm bleed on all four sides (background colours run to 106 × 78 mm). Keep all text at least 4 mm inside the trim line.
Colour
Navy#0A2240 Sky#3FA9E0 Gold#D9A62E

These are screen values. Hand the hex codes to the printer and let them convert with their own press profile — a CMYK conversion done here would be a guess at their ink and stock. If colour accuracy matters across reprints, ask for a Pantone match on the navy; it is the one that carries the brand.

Resolution Supply as vector PDF where possible. The logo here is a raster image at 360 px across a 12 mm space — about 760 dpi, fine for this size, but a vector version of the badge would print cleaner and is worth commissioning once.
Variable data Group and CCA change per model. For a run covering several batteries, send the printer one file per group size — generate each by editing CONFIG and printing again.
Legibility Group digits are 22 mm tall and CCA 15 mm, both readable at arm's length in poor light. The warning text is set at 2 mm, which is the smallest that stays legible after lamination.

One thing to confirm before ordering: the warning wording here is what you supplied. Battery labelling is regulated, and the required phrasing can differ by jurisdiction and by whether you are reselling or relabelling. Worth a call to your supplier or to Alberta Environment before a large run — reprinting a thousand labels costs more than the phone call.
