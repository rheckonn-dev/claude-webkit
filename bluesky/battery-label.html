BlueSky battery label

Adhesive label for unbranded batteries. Group size and CCA are the two things a customer looks for, so they carry the layout. Every measurement is a share of the label height — change labelW and labelH in the CONFIG block at the top of this file and the whole design rescales with it.

1 · Turn it and look at it

Arrástrala para girar · Drag to rotate

Case drawn at group-35 proportions — 230 mm wide, 225 mm tall including the terminal deck. Terminal positions are illustrative; which side the positive post sits on varies by group, so this is not a polarity reference.

2 · Actual size

Check the scale before ordering. The label is drawn in millimetres, so a printout at 100% — no “fit to page” — measures exactly. If the ruler's end mark lands anywhere but its stated width, the print scaling is off, not the file.

Legibility

Confirm the warning wording before a large run. Battery labelling is regulated and the required phrasing can differ by jurisdiction and by whether you are reselling or relabelling. A call to your supplier or to Alberta Environment costs less than reprinting a thousand labels.
