BlueSky battery labels

Two labels: one for the case face carrying the group size, CCA and the regulatory warning, and a smaller one for the lid whose only real job is the phone number. Every measurement is a share of each label's height — change the dimensions in the CONFIG block at the top of this file and the designs rescale with them.

Case drawn at group-35 proportions. Terminal positions are illustrative; which side the positive post sits on varies by group, so this is not a polarity reference.

2 · Actual size

Check the scale before ordering. Both labels are drawn in millimetres, so a printout at 100% — no “fit to page” — measures exactly. If the ruler's end mark lands anywhere but its stated width, the print scaling is off, not the file.

Legibility

Confirm the warning wording before a large run. Battery labelling is regulated and the required phrasing can differ by jurisdiction and by whether you are reselling or relabelling. The French here is a faithful rendering of the English, but if these ship into Quebec the wording may be prescribed rather than translated — worth one call to your supplier.
